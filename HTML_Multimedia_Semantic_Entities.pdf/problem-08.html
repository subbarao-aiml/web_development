Financial Tips & Account Opening

  • Maintain an emergency fund equal to > 6 months of expenses .
  • Pay off high-interest credit card balance first .
  • Diversify investments across mutual funds and fixed deposits .

Steps to Open a Savings Account

  1. Choose a banking product matching your needs .
  2. Gather identity and address verification documents .
  3. Fill out the online callback / application request .
  4. Complete Video-KYC verification .
