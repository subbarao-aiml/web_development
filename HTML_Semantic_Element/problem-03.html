Healthcare Terms

Consultation
Medical discussion and examination with a healthcare professional.
Follow-up
A subsequent visit to monitor treatment or recovery.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time for meeting a doctor or healthcare professional.
