Event Overview

About the Career & Education Fair

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 connects students, graduates, job seekers and employers with exciting career and education opportunities.

Participants can explore placements, internships, scholarships, apprenticeships and higher education.

The event is designed to help young professionals discover the right career path.

Students are encouraged to prepare their resumes and carry valid identification documents.

Registration is required for attending the event.

Preparation Guide

Tips for Attendees

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Carry a valid identity card.
  • Dress professionally.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive the event confirmation.
  3. Attend the registration desk.
  4. Visit participating organizations.
  5. Attend workshops and presentations.
  6. Interact with recruiters.
  7. Submit applications where appropriate.

Important Career Terms

Career Fair
An event where employers and candidates meet to discuss career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary work experience opportunity for students and graduates.
Placement
A process through which students or graduates obtain employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to students for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training program combining practical work experience with learning.

Important Information

Participants should arrive at least 30 minutes before their selected session.

Please carry your resume in both printed and digital formats.

Registration confirmation can be stored using the registration_id provided after submission. 

Example:
Registration ID : NCEF2026-001
Attendee Type   : Student
Preferred Sector: Information Technology

Participants may attend multiple sessions depending on availability. The event follows a maximum capacity policy for selected workshops.

For example, a workshop with capacity of 100 participants represents 100 × 1 available seats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration mandatory?

Yes. Participants should register before attending the career fair.

Can students attend?

Yes. Students, graduates and job seekers can participate.

Should I bring my resume?

Yes. It is recommended to carry multiple copies of your updated resume.

Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes, participants can select their preferred sessions during registration.

Is there an entry fee?

Event registration is free unless a specific workshop states otherwise.
