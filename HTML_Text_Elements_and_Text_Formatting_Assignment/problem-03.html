City Library Opens New Digital Learning Centre

The city library has announced the opening of a new digital learning centre, which will provide residents with access to a wide range of online resources and educational materials. and this is useful for the students for their laptop work and their assignments .students and faculties can be learned here . The centre has been designed to give students and residents a quiet place to study, explore technology, and attend free learning sessions. The Central City Library opened its new Digital Learning Centre on august 29th,2026

The centre includes twenty public computers and a dedicated area for beginner programming workshops. Library staff said that the facility will remain open after school hours so that more learners can use it.

The new centre is not only about computers; it is about giving every learner a chance to grow, said the library coordinator. During the opening event, local teachers welcomed the project and encouraged students to visit regularly.
