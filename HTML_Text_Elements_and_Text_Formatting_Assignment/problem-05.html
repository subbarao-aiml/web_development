Science Notes

Chemistry

Water formula is H2O.

Carbon dioxide formula is CO2.

Oxygen gas formula is O2.

Sulfuric acid formula is H2SO4.

Methane formula is CH4.

Chemical formulas show the elements and atom counts in a substance.

Physics

The SI unit of area is metre squared: m2.

The SI unit of volume is metre cubed: m3.

One million can be written as 106.

Force can change an object's motion or shape.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using sunlight.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.

Quick Revision

Use subscript in chemical formulas and superscript for powers.
