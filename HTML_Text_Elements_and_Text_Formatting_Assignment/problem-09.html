Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their college identity card while on campus. The identity card must be shown when requested by staff.

Students should keep classrooms and common areas clean. Respect for shared spaces helps everyone learn comfortably.

Attendance

A minimum attendance of 75% is required for every subject.

Low attendance may affect examination eligibility. Students should contact their department if they are absent for several days.

Examination

Students must follow all examination hall instructions.
Mobile phones, notes, and unauthorised materials are not allowed.

Students should arrive at least fifteen minutes before the examination begins.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted in the format and method given by the instructor.

Late submissions after the deadline may receive a penalty. Students should keep a copy of their completed assignment.

Discipline

Students must behave respectfully toward teachers, staff, and classmates.

Bullying, harassment, and damage to college property are not allowed. Students can ask their department for guidance if they do not understand a rule.
