College Hosts Technology Exhibition

Students Present New Technology Projects

A major technology exhibition was held at ABC College. Students from different departments participated in the event.

The exhibition took place in the college auditorium. Many students displayed their technology projects.

The event started at 10:00 AM and continued throughout the day. Teachers and students visited the exhibition.

The main purpose of the event was to encourage students to develop creativity and practical skills.
