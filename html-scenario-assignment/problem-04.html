Jaipur Travel Guide

Attractions

Jaipur is famous for Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort and City Palace.

Culture

Jaipur has a rich culture with traditional music, dance, festivals and handicrafts.

Food

Popular foods include Dal Baati Churma and Ghewar.

Best Time

The best season to visit Jaipur is October to March.

Travel Tips

Carry water and comfortable clothes while travelling.
